# OPENING HOURS
- Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM
- Saturday and Sunday: 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM
- Public Holidays: Closed
- Special Extended Hours during Exam Season: Open until Midnight (Monday to Friday only)

# BORROWING POLICIES
- Undergraduate students: Up to 10 books for 14 days.
- Postgraduate and final-year students: Up to 20 books for 30 days.
- Faculty: Up to 50 books for 90 days.
- Equipment Loans: Postgraduate and final-year students can borrow a loaner laptop for up to 3 days, including weekends. Pick it up at the Help desk with your student ID; late returns are 50 rupees per day.
- Renewals: Books can be renewed once online via the student portal if no hold requests exist.

# LOCATIONS & COLLECTIONS
- Floor 1 (Ground): Help Desk, Quick Search Computers, New Arrivals, Cafe Corner.
- Floor 2: Reference Section (Bays B-12 to B-16), Computer Science & Engineering books (Bays B-1 to B-11), Study Rooms.
  - "Introduction to Algorithms" by Cormen et al. is located on Floor 2, Aisle B, Bay 14. Call number QA76.6 .C66.
  - "Clean Code" by Robert C. Martin is on Floor 2, Aisle B, Bay 5. Call number QA76.73 .C66.
- Floor 3: Humanities, Social Sciences, Quiet Study Area, Tamil Literature Section.
- Floor 4: Medical and Biological Sciences, Research Journals, Periodical Room.

# E-RESOURCES
- We have access to IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, and ACS Publications.
- JSTOR access is available for Humanities and Social Sciences students.
- For recent papers on solid-state batteries, we recommend checking "Nature Energy", "Journal of Power Sources" via IEEE, or "ACS Energy Letters".
- Off-campus access is available via university VPN. Contact it.help@meridian.edu for VPN credentials.

# ADDITIONAL SERVICES
- Printing and Photocopying: Available on Floors 1 and 3 (5 rupees per page, color 10 rupees per page).
- Interlibrary Loan: Requests can be made online via the student portal. Delivery takes 3-5 business days. Free up to 5 requests per semester.
- Locker Rental: Available at the entrance for 10 rupees per day with your student ID.
- Lost and Found: Managed at the Help Desk on Floor 1.
