# University Library Code of Conduct and Policies

## 1. Quiet Zones
The entire 3rd and 4th floors are strictly designated as **Quiet Zones**. Conversations must be kept to an absolute minimum. Phone calls are not permitted in these areas.

## 2. Food and Drink
- **Drinks**: Permitted only in spill-proof, covered containers.
- **Food**: Cold snacks are permitted in designated lounge areas. Hot, messy, or strong-smelling foods are strictly prohibited everywhere inside the library.
- **Computers**: No food or drink is allowed at the library computer terminals.

## 3. Study Rooms
Study rooms can be booked up to 2 weeks in advance. A minimum of 2 people is required to book a large study room. If you do not arrive within 15 minutes of your booking time, your reservation will be canceled.

## 4. Borrowing Privileges
You must present a valid University ID to borrow any materials. Alumni and guests may apply for a special visitor card at the circulation desk.
