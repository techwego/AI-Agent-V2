MERIDIAN UNIVERSITY CENTRAL LIBRARY - GENERAL INFORMATION

# OPENING HOURS
- Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM
- Saturday and Sunday: 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM
- Holidays: Closed

# BORROWING POLICIES
- Undergraduate students: Up to 10 books for 14 days.
- Postgraduate and final-year students: Up to 20 books for 30 days.
- Faculty: Up to 50 books for 90 days.
- Equipment Loans: Postgraduate and final-year students can borrow a loaner laptop for up to 3 days, including weekends. Pick it up at the Help desk with your student ID; late returns are 50 rupees per day.

# LOCATIONS & COLLECTIONS
- Floor 1 (Ground): Help Desk, Quick Search Computers, New Arrivals.
- Floor 2: Reference Section (Bays B-12 to B-16), Computer Science & Engineering books (Bays B-1 to B-11), Study Rooms.
  - "Introduction to Algorithms" by Cormen et al. is located on Floor 2, Aisle B, Bay 14. Call number QA76.6 .C66.
- Floor 3: Humanities, Social Sciences, Quiet Study Area.
- Floor 4: Medical and Biological Sciences, Research Journals.

# E-RESOURCES
- We have access to IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, and ACS Publications.
- For recent papers on solid-state batteries, we recommend checking "Nature Energy", "Journal of Power Sources" via IEEE, or "ACS Energy Letters".

# ADDITIONAL SERVICES
- Printing and Photocopying: Available on Floors 1 and 3 (5 rupees per page).
- Interlibrary Loan: Requests can be made online via the student portal. Delivery takes 3-5 business days.
